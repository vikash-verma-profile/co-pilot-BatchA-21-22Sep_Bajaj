BAJAJ ELECTRICALS — WARRANTY & SERVICE POLICY (TRAINING EXCERPT)
Document for classroom use only — illustrative, not legal policy.

1. Coverage
- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship for the stated warranty period on the product packaging / warranty card.
- Warranty starts from date of purchase invoice.
- Valid only for products sold through authorized Bajaj Electricals channels in India.

2. Claim process
1) Customer contacts authorized service center / call center with invoice and product serial/model.
2) Service team registers complaint and provides ticket number.
3) Diagnosis completed; if manufacturing defect confirmed within warranty, repair or replacement as per policy matrix.
4) Customer confirmation and ticket closure.

3. Turnaround guidance (indicative)
- Metro cities: diagnosis within 3 working days where parts available.
- Other locations: diagnosis within 5–7 working days subject to logistics.

4. Exclusions
- Damage due to improper installation, unauthorized repair, power surge without protection (where applicable), misuse, or normal wear and tear.
- Commercial use of household-rated products unless explicitly covered.
- Missing invoice / tampered serial labels may lead to claim rejection.
- Consumables and aesthetic parts may have limited or no coverage.

5. Customer responsibilities
- Retain invoice.
- Use authorized installation where mandated (e.g., certain water heaters / large appliances).
- Provide safe access for technician visits.

6. Escalation
- If unresolved beyond published TAT, escalate via call center with ticket number to regional service manager.
